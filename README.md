# Backpack Keybinds — Issues & Suggestions

Found a bug or have an idea? This is the public issue tracker for **Backpack Keybinds**, an addon for Sophisticated Backpacks.

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/ThiccTapeman/backpack-keybinds-issues/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
- [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/ThiccTapeman/backpack-keybinds-issues/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
- [Search existing reports](https://github.com/ThiccTapeman/backpack-keybinds-issues/issues?q=is%3Aissue)
- [Download on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/backpack-keybinds)

## Reporting a bug

Include your Minecraft, Backpack Keybinds, mod loader, Sophisticated Backpacks, and Sophisticated Core versions. Explain how to reproduce the problem, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Mention whether you play in singleplayer or multiplayer, whether the server has Backpack Keybinds installed, and whether the affected backpack is carried, nested, or placed. Logs and screenshots are welcome when helpful.

Reports and attachments are public. Remove private details from logs before uploading them.
